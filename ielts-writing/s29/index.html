ĐỀ 1 | TOPIC 4.2 | IELTS Writing Task 2

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

Write about the following topic:

With the rapid development of communication technology; e.g. smart phones, tablets and other mobile devices, some people believe that the disadvantages outweigh the advantages. 

To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

ĐỀ 2 | TOPIC 4.5 | IELTS Writing Task 2

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

Write about the following topic:

Some people think that the increasing use of computers and mobile phones for communication has a negative effect on young people’s reading and writing skills. 

To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

ĐỀ 3 | TOPIC 4.6 | IELTS Writing Task 2

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

Write about the following topic:

The Internet has transformed the way information is shared and consumed, but it has also created problems that did not exist before.

What are the most serious problems associated with the Internet and what are the possible solutions?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.
