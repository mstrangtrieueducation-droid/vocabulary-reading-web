ĐỀ 1 | TOPIC 9.2.1 | IELTS Writing Task 2

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

Write about the following topic:

The government should invest more money in teaching science than other subjects for a country’s development and progress.  

To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

ĐỀ 2 | TOPIC 9.6 | IELTS Writing Task 2

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

Write about the following topic:

Many people argue that in order to improve educational quality, high school students should be encouraged to make comments or even criticism on their teachers. Others; however, think it will lead to loss of respect and discipline in the classroom.

Discuss both views and give your opinion.

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

ĐỀ 3 | TOPIC 9.9.1 | IELTS Writing Task 2

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

Write about the following topic:

Schools are no longer necessary because children can get so much information available through the Internet, and they can study just as well at home.

To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.
